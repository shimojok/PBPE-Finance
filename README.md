# 📘 **PBPE-Finance**

### _Financial Layer of the Planetary Bio‑Positive Economy_

**Transforming Biosecurity, Soil Carbon, and Food System Stability into Investable Assets**

---

## 🌍 1. Introduction

**PBPE-Finance** is the **financial structuring engine** of the  
**Planetary Bio‑Positive Economy (PBPE)**.

Its mission is simple and radical:

> **Turn microbial ecology into global finance.**  
> **Turn soil regeneration into yield.**  
> **Turn yield into credits.**  
> **Turn credits into capital flows.**

PBPE-Finance converts biological improvements generated by:

- **MBT-Biosecurity-Engine**（12 KPIs）
- **AGRIX-OS**（phenomics, yield, soil, climate）
- **HealthBook-AI**（One Health, antibiotic reduction）
- **MBT Probiotics**（livestock biosecurity）

into **financial assets, credits, and investment products**.

---

# 🧬 2. The PBPE Triple-Ledger System

### _Green × Blue × Gold = A New Asset Class_

PBPE-Finance integrates **three economic ledgers** into a single investable system.

|Ledger|Asset|Source Engine|
|---|---|---|
|**Green (Carbon)**|ΔC (soil carbon), CH₄/N₂O reduction|MBT-Biosecurity-Engine|
|**Blue (Health)**|Avoided healthcare cost, antibiotic reduction|HealthBook-AI|
|**Gold (Productivity)**|Yield gain, quality premium, food loss reduction|AGRIX-OS|

This creates the world’s first **multi-dimensional climate asset**.

# 💡 3. The Negative Green Premium

### _Where sustainability becomes cheaper than destruction_

Bill Gates defined the **Green Premium** as  
“the extra cost of choosing a clean alternative.”

PBPE-Finance introduces the opposite:

> **Negative Green Premium**  
> Sustainability is _profitable_, not costly.

Example (Coffee, per hectare):

|Item|Cost / Value|
|---|---|
|Chemical fertilizer|**$120**|
|MBT55 biofertilizer|**$19**|
|SOC carbon revenue|**$45**|
|Yield gain|**+30–50%**|
|Disease loss avoided|**85–100%**|

**Net Premium = −$146/ha**  
Profit, not cost.

# 📊 5. PBPE Credit System（5種類のクレジット）

PBPE-Finance manages **five classes of PBPE Credits**.

---

## **5.1 Biosecurity Credits（中心資産）**

Generated from MBT-Biosecurity-Engine’s 12 KPIs:

- Disease Loss Reduction
- Anti-Spoilage
- Yield Gain
- Quality Premium
- Livestock Biosecurity
- Food Loss Reduction

These credits represent **risk reduction + productivity gains**.

---

## **5.2 Carbon Credits（ΔC + GHG）**

- Soil carbon sequestration（ΔC）
- CH₄ reduction
- N₂O reduction

These are **MRV-ready climate assets**.

---

## **5.3 Food Loss Credits**

Generated from:

- Anti-Spoilage
- Marketable yield increase
- Shelf-life extension

These credits reduce **Scope 3 emissions** for buyers.

---

## **5.4 Quality Credits**

Generated from:

- Brix
- Polyphenols
- Defect rate

Used by:

- Specialty coffee buyers
- Premium cacao buyers
- Tea & wine industries

---

## **5.5 Price Stability Credits（世界初）**

Generated from:

- Price Stability Index
- Yield CV reduction
- Supply chain resilience

These credits reduce **market volatility risk**.

- Price volatility

- Risk/return

## **6.4 climate_credit_model.py**

- ΔC → tCO₂
- CH₄ → tCO₂e

- Climate credit valuation

## **6.5 price_stability_model.py**

Uses:

- Yield CV
- Price volatility

Outputs:

- Price Stability Credit value
